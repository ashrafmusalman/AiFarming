🌾 About the App — Ai Farming Platform

Ai Farming is a modern agricultural marketplace and AI-powered assistant designed to help farmers manage daily activities with ease.
From buying & selling produce to checking live market prices and getting AI-based farming help — everything is available in one place.

⭐ Key Features
🛒 Buy & Sell Farm Products

Farmers can easily buy and sell vegetables, fruits, and other agricultural items.
Clear product cards display price, description, and seller information.

📊 Live State-wise Vegetable Prices

Get real-time price updates from different Indian states to help farmers sell at the right time and maximize profit.

👤 Farmer Profile Dashboard

Each farmer gets a personalized dashboard showing:

Total products bought

Total products sold


Market insights

🤖 AI Farming Chatbot

A built-in chatbot designed only for farming-related questions.
Farmers can ask about:

Crops & soil

Fertilizers

Market prices

Farming techniques

The AI provides fast, accurate answers to help with daily farm planning.

🚜 Simple, Easy-to-Use Interface

Designed especially for farmers — clean UI, big icons, and easy navigation for all age groups.
